Perhaps because the affair of years past was long ago, Shen Yu's loathing for Qingmeng had eased. Shen Yu patted Qingmeng's shoulder and smiled: "Mengmeng grows prettier the older you get. When you reach your hairpin ceremony, your father will surely choose a husband for you to your liking." But Shen Yu did not know that Qingmeng was no longer the little girl who longed for a crumb of fatherly affection, as in years past. Qingmeng shook off Shen Yu's hand and ran straight to Meng Yu. "Mother, don't be sad... Even without Father, Daodao and I will be filial to you..." Meng Yu's drooping eyes suddenly lit up: "Mengmeng, what did you just call me?" Qingmeng pressed lips together, a little nervous in the heart: "Mother..." Meng Yu answered with joy and hugged Qingmeng tightly. The sorrow on Meng Yu's face was swept clean away by happiness, as if the troubles of a moment ago had never mattered at all. Qingmeng held Meng Yu's thin shoulders. Tears rolled down in silence. On the day Qingmeng dueled in martial arts and defeated Meng Yu, Meng Yu suddenly burst into loud laughter: "Good! Good! Mengmeng, I truly did not misjudge you!" By then, Qingmeng resembled Meng Yu of the old days: outstanding in talent, enchanting in looks, with fame spreading across the capital. Shen Yu began to value Qingmeng more, even far above the children born of the concubines' rooms afterward. Shen Dao was sincerely happy for Qingmeng, but could not help worrying: "Jiejie, tomorrow is your hairpin ceremony. I don't want you married off the moment you come of age..." Influenced by Meng Yu, the girls had long since stopped being bound by the doctrines of the age. Qingmeng pinched Shen Dao's cheek lightly and smiled with confidence: "Even if Father is determined to marry me off, so what? Can't I run away with Mother and you?" "And me too!" Xue Lin's voice rang out from behind. Xue Lin ran up, beaming: "Why didn't you invite me along?" The three looked at one another and burst into ringing laughter. The hairpin ceremony came quickly. Shen Yu wanted to marry Qingmeng to the eldest son of Grand General Yang. Qingmeng refused flatly before all the guests. Eyes sweeping Young General Yang from head to toe, Qingmeng curled a lip in mockery: "An ignorant fool with only an empty name. How is he worthy of me?" The words were barely out when the whole room changed color. Shen Yu slammed a hand on the table and rose in fury: "What are you doing! Have you no sense of decorum!" Qingmeng smiled and hurled the dagger in hand straight at Young General Yang, shattering the teacup before Young General Yang. Young General Yang jolted and fell backward onto the floor. Qingmeng spoke coolly: "A man like this, I will never marry." Qingmeng's manner was perfectly calm, yet a memory flickered through the mind. Years ago, when Meng Yu had charged alone into the estate to propose marriage to Shen Yu, perhaps Meng Yu had been every bit as commanding as Qingmeng was now. On the very second day after the hairpin ceremony, Meng Yu vanished, leaving only a letter. "Mengmeng, you have grown up and can protect yourself now, so Mother can rest easy entrusting Daodao to you." "I am truly too tired. I do not want to spend the rest of my life planted in this Shen estate, nor face your father day after day." "He thinks he can use a little old affection to bind me, and use this house to cage me, but he is wrong." "Men are nothing but passersby in our lives. When they have no more value, we cast them aside. The only ones I cannot bear to leave are you and Daodao." "Now I will go wandering across the rivers and mountains and enjoy the rest of my life." "Mother will return to visit you. Carrier pigeons will keep us in touch. Don't worry, I have prepared plenty of money, and everything is fine." "The dowry and the money I have saved over the years are all in the storehouse. I leave the keys to you two. Use that money to do whatever you wish." Signed: Meng Yu. The handwriting was still graceful and forceful, like a dragon in flight and a phoenix in dance. Shen Dao nestled against Qingmeng and sobbed. Qingmeng stroked Shen Dao's hair to comfort, yet in the heart felt glad for Meng Yu. Meng Yu had remained Meng Yu all along. A free eagle would never allow anyone to cage it. With the money Meng Yu left behind, Qingmeng founded a community home. The place took in girls adrift and stranded, women who had divorced and had nowhere to turn, and even elderly women near the end of life with no one to care for the elderly. Shen Yu cursed Qingmeng as mad, but Qingmeng did not mind. Qingmeng and Shen Dao began helping these women find a way to live again. Qingmeng spent a great deal of money to hire embroiderers, dancers, tutors, cooks, martial masters... In short, Qingmeng recruited the finest in every trade. Following each woman's gifts, Qingmeng guided each woman's studies. Those who wished to learn letters followed the tutor. From not knowing a single character, the women gradually came to write fluent essays and speak with ease. Those who loved handicrafts studied embroidery under artisans, made goods to sell, and the money earned sustained this special school. Those who loved cooking enthusiastically invented new dishes. From roadside food carts, the women gradually rented a tavern, and the fame of the women's restaurant spread far and wide. Even the singing girls and dancers once despised by the world became respected female teachers. The women taught the craft to students and together opened proper venues for performance. Martial arts alone was a required subject for everyone. Qingmeng always remembered the words of Meng Yu: a girl who knows martial arts for self-defense can never have too much of it. These women had all once been destitute, enduring bitterness and the contempt of a cruel world, and so a fierce longing took root in the heart of each. The women wanted to prove to the whole world that the women were no useless creatures, and could even do better than men. After only three years, the school founded by Qingmeng and Shen Dao grew ever larger and rescued countless women. More and more people awakened. Threatened in status, the men banded together to submit memorials to the Emperor, demanding punishment. But the men did not understand that the Emperor was a person too. And people always weigh gain against loss. Over these three years, Qingmeng had taken the initiative to hand half of the school's income to the Emperor. Everything the school did had received the Emperor's tacit permission. Only the conservative officials did not understand, or refused to believe. The officials did not believe women could be so talented. The officials did not believe that anyone among the officials could ever be surpassed. Qingmeng stood in the study and bowed deeply in respectful salute: "Your Majesty, this time your humble subject comes for the sake of the realm, and also for the thousands upon thousands of women under heaven." The Emperor looked at Qingmeng and said nothing. "In recent years the borders have been invaded again and again by foreign tribes. This humble subject begs to lead three thousand female soldiers into battle and drive off the Xiongnu." Qingmeng took out a small square block of rice cake: "As for provisions, this humble subject has an important matter to report." "This is a new dry ration developed by us. It is very light and keeps for up to six months. Eaten dry or soaked in water, it fills the belly and restores strength quickly." With that, Qingmeng presented several memorials: "Your Majesty, calamities both natural and man-made have struck without end lately. This humble subject cannot bear to see Your Majesty exhaust mind and strength, and so has drafted several solutions, begging Your Majesty to review them." Holding the dry ration in one hand and offering the memorials with the other, Qingmeng knelt prostrate on the floor, heart pounding. "Quick! Bring both of those up here!" The Emperor, ever composed, could not hide the excitement. The Emperor had a eunuch take the items from Qingmeng's hands, then hurriedly broke off a corner of the cake. The food-tasting eunuch stepped forward at once to try it. The eunuch's eyes lit up: "Your Majesty, there is no poison in this!" The Emperor broke off a piece too and put it in the mouth. The faint sweetness of glutinous rice melted on the tip of the tongue, bringing a satisfied nod. After that, the Emperor opened the memorials to read. The Emperor was a little surprised: "The handwriting of a woman, and yet so free and graceful?" With that, the Emperor kept reading, gradually becoming wholly absorbed. Qingmeng did not know how long Qingmeng had knelt, only that the knees had gone numb and the spine trembled with fatigue. At last the Emperor spoke: "Shen Qingmeng!" "This subject is here!" Cold sweat seeped from Qingmeng's brow. Qingmeng feared that this capricious sovereign would turn to blame. After all, there had never been a precedent for a woman to interfere in court affairs. But Qingmeng still wished to gamble once. If it failed, Qingmeng alone would bear it. And if it succeeded? Qingmeng wanted to open a road to life for thousands upon thousands of women in the world. Time passed heavily, bit by bit. Just as Qingmeng was about to be unable to hold on, the Emperor suddenly burst into loud laughter: "I truly did not expect women could be this capable!" "Su Jinsheng! Draft an imperial edict at once and confer office on these people!" "Proclaim my decree: From this day forward, official posts are formally opened to women. Women may take the civil examinations alongside men, and those who pass shall receive fully equal treatment!" Qingmeng looked up in astonishment, unable to believe the ears. The Emperor waved a hand at Qingmeng: "Rise. Your request is granted." "I appoint you General of Agile Cavalry, to lead the female soldiers to the border to fight, and bring glory to the dynasty!" Two years later, in a battle beyond the border, Qingmeng led the female troops back in great victory. On the streets, there were no longer only women in coarse cloth, but countless female officials in court robes, with winged caps upon the head. From the windows of the taverns, exquisitely made-up dancers leaned out and smiled brightly, tossing brilliant sprigs of flowers down upon the marching troops. Waiters from the women's restaurants darted about, handing out sweet cakes to celebrate the victory. The streets were boisterous, men and women alike in high spirits. From afar, students from the schools waved to welcome the troops. Qingmeng saw many familiar faces on both sides of the road, every face bright with smiles. "Jiejie!" Suddenly, Qingmeng heard Shen Dao calling. Looking up, Qingmeng saw Shen Dao leaning half out of a second-floor tavern window, waving eagerly. Seated beside Shen Dao was a middle-aged woman, watching quietly. Mother. Qingmeng's breath caught, and then joy burst forth and flooded the mind. Qingmeng looked at Meng Yu. Meng Yu smiled and nodded, eyes full of approval. Qingmeng waved hard in return: "Mother! I did it!" Qingmeng had done it. On that night years ago, Meng Yu had whispered a wish that the world might have more women like Meng Yu, or at the very least women more capable than Meng Yu. Today, Qingmeng had done it. This brilliant era, Mother, you have seen with your own eyes. In this world, women were no longer creatures dependent on men. Women were like Mother, like Qingmeng, like Shen Dao. Each must first be oneself, and only then a woman.
