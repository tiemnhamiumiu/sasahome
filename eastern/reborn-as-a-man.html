One day, on the way to court, they finally succeeded on Zhuque Street. The tearing pain of death came once more. It truly hurt... In the next life, would Gu Xiuhe be a man or a woman? Gu Xiuhe asked the self. Being a man would be so good, because Gu Xiuhe would still be Gu Xiuhe, and by changing the sex alone could walk into court and become Chancellor, living a brilliant and splendid life. But... Could it be that because being a woman was too hard, Gu Xiuhe would no longer want to be one? If everyone thought that way, the world would have collapsed long ago. If Gu Xiuhe did the same, how would that differ from a coward? The weeping of women echoed in the ears. Gu Xiuhe heard the voice of Yuan Mingyue, of Si Wenming... If Gu Xiuhe died, what would become of them? Would the things Gu Xiuhe had done still have any meaning? Gu Xiuhe sank into deep sleep with countless questions in the heart. "Elder Sister! Elder Sister, wake up!" "Why is Xiuhe dozing off again?" Gu Xiuhe heard the voice of the Marchioness. "You are the eldest sister. If you lie sleeping like this, the younger ones will learn from you! How will you manage the next test? It would be strange if Teacher Si did not punish you!" Gu Xiuhe awoke with a start. Still in the schoolroom. Gu Xiuhe looked at the younger sisters holding books and reading, then up at the platform where Si Wenming stood, and by instinct raised a hand to touch the chest. A woman. "What are you doing!" The Marchioness pulled the hand down. "The Crown Princess has arrived, and you still do not hurry out to welcome her, do you mean to be discourteous!" ... Crown Princess? ... Who was that? Full of doubt, Gu Xiuhe stepped out of the schoolroom and saw, in the gentle spring wind, tall Yuan Mingyue standing straight beside a peach tree. "Would you like to go see the lantern festival?" Yuan Mingyue flushed and held out a cool object to Gu Xiuhe. It was the jade butterfly. Gu Xiuhe's nose stung suddenly, and the throat choked. "... So it truly did have meaning." "What did you say?" Gu Xiuhe pinned the jade butterfly into the hair, and took Yuan Mingyue's hand of Gu Xiuhe's own accord. "Let us go! There is still a long road ahead, waiting for us!" Two lifetimes of searching had been spent only to trade for today. Not a moment of this lovely spring must be wasted.
