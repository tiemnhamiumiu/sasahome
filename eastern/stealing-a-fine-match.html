In the hunt, the sister had shone brilliantly, at ease and unfettered like a crane spreading wings across the high sky, and the display had drawn everyone's admiration. Jiang Yan had secretly wished that someday it might be possible to be like the sister and escape the cramped back courtyard of the Duke's Estate. Unfortunately Jiang Yan's body had been frail since childhood and could not train in martial arts, so there was nothing to do but ride around the safe area. Who could have known that the horse would suddenly spook and throw Jiang Yan to the ground. There was not a soul around, and the horse had long since vanished from sight. Jiang Yan's ankle was dislocated, making walking impossible, so there was nothing to do but sit still in one place and wait for rescue. As night fell, the temperature dropped suddenly. Jiang Yan kept sneezing, shivering, and the ankle had already swollen into a big lump. Jiang Yan could only pray silently that no wild beasts would appear in this outer area. "Who is there?" Jiang Yan hurriedly picked up a dry branch for self-defense. A soft laugh sounded: "So it is the Second Miss of the Jiang family. Come here and help me up a bit." It was Pei Qingyuan, the young master of the Pei family. A youth with black hair tied high, wearing a long robe of embroidered brocade, splendid in its patterns. But the robes were torn in several places, and the look was thoroughly bedraggled. It seemed Pei Qingyuan had been hurt. Dragging the aching leg closer with effort, Jiang Yan supported Pei Qingyuan. The arms of Pei Qingyuan were quite badly scraped, though fortunately not too serious. Jiang Yan used what little knowledge there was to give first aid. "You are skilled at this too, little sister?" Pei Qingyuan joked. "My elder sister trains in martial arts and is often hurt, so I had to learn to bandage her." The night wind blew icy cold, and Jiang Yan sneezed several more times. Suddenly a warm cloak was draped over Jiang Yan's shoulders. The youth said nothing and simply gazed quietly into the distance: "Let us go." Jiang Yan was dazed: "Go where?" Pei Qingyuan smiled, a smile so beautiful it made the heart ache: "To the camp, of course. I will carry you." Jiang Yan was flustered and did not know what to do. Pei Qingyuan had already crouched down at some point and tilted up the head to look at Jiang Yan: "Do you trust me?" For some reason, Jiang Yan nodded. So Jiang Yan obediently climbed onto that back and let Pei Qingyuan carry Jiang Yan safely back to camp. The scenery on the road that night Jiang Yan no longer remembered clearly. Only the moon that night remained, round and bright. Later, Pei Qingyuan grew ever more outstanding, fame ringing through the whole capital. Beside Jiang Yan's lively, radiant elder sister, Pei Qingyuan made a perfect pair, truly a match made in heaven. Back then Jiang Yan had thought that this might be exactly what the Buddhist scriptures often called "seeking and not finding." On the fifth day after the mourning bells sounded, a sudden chaos of noise exploded outside. The palace maids and eunuchs inside the hall rushed to surround Jiang Yan and the Crown Prince at the center. Cui Tao, uneasy, took out a set of palace maid clothing: "Miss, let this servant change your clothes for you!" What a silly girl. If the ones bursting in were not relief troops, no one inside could escape death, and changing clothes would solve nothing. Still, Jiang Yan was deeply moved by the girl's devotion: "Do you trust me?" Cui Tao, who had grown up with Jiang Yan from childhood, had eyes already red, and nodded quickly. "Then that is good." Cui Tao trusted Jiang Yan, and Jiang Yan trusted Pei Qingyuan. The great door was kicked open. Half of the soldiers guarding outside had fallen, and fresh blood had stained the whole courtyard before the hall red. A commander in iron armor led a troop with sharp blades in hand into the hall. The remaining guards did all possible to hold the intruders off, the clash of swords and the chilling screams driving straight into the ears. The commander took a step forward, and a gaze swept the room and came to rest on the Crown Prince, held tight in Jiang Yan's arms. The commander gave a cold order, the long scar on the face growing more savage: "Keep the Crown Prince. Kill all the rest, spare no one!" Cui Tao hurriedly stood in front of Jiang Yan, while the Crown Prince clung tight to Jiang Yan's neck and would not let go. Suddenly a loud sound rang out. The commander who had just given the order spat out a mouthful of fresh blood, a long sword thrust through the chest. The commander collapsed to the ground on the spot. Jiang Yan let out a breath of relief and lifted a gaze toward the hall door. It was Pei Qingyuan. Pei Qingyuan had come! Just like that year in the dangerous hunt, Pei Qingyuan appeared bedraggled, soaked in fresh blood. But in Jiang Yan's eyes, there was no one in the world more flawless than Pei Qingyuan at this moment. "The Prince plotted rebellion, coerced the palace, and sought to harm the imperial family. His crimes are monstrous, he deceived those above and below, and his treason cannot be forgiven. Execute him on the spot!" One order from Pei Qingyuan, and every remnant of the Prince's men was wiped out at once. Pei Qingyuan walked over, light from behind spilling across the figure as Jiang Yan was pulled into a tight embrace: "It is all right now." The world around seemed to settle into stillness. The tangled slaughter outside seemed to have nothing more to do with Jiang Yan and Pei Qingyuan. Jiang Yan savored the peace and warmth of this moment, until the little Crown Prince clinging to Jiang Yan's neck let out a coaxing cry: "Shimu... save me... I am about to stop breathing!" By the time the Grand Princess arrived, Huayang Hall had been cleaned up completely. Cui Tao stood to one side, still not recovered from the shock. The Grand Princess did not bother with many words, only rolled the eyes once and left behind one sentence: "If you are done being affectionate, hurry back to Qinzheng Hall and receive the command orders! I am busy to death!" The little Crown Prince, seated between Jiang Yan and Pei Qingyuan, suddenly blurted out: "Auntie seems a bit fierce." Pei Qingyuan gave a soft snicker: "She is jealous!" Jiang Yan glanced at Pei Qingyuan and said nothing. As if remembering something, Pei Qingyuan reached out and toyed with a loose lock of hair on Jiang Yan's shoulder, eyes full of curiosity: "A-Yan, how did you see through the play between the Grand Princess and me?" This man, having gained the advantage, was still pretending to be innocent. "If the Grand Princess truly meant to rebel, why would she go to the trouble of moving all the longtime servants of the Eastern Palace to Huayang Palace? Moreover, if she planned to become a female sovereign as in a previous dynasty, then after His Majesty passed away she would absolutely not have spared the Crown Prince's life. But she not only did not act, she doubled the guards outside Huayang Palace. That is not house arrest. It is protection in secret." "As for the war in the Northwest," Jiang Yan took a sip of tea and went on calmly: "you deliberately told Qing Zhu to spread false rumors, did you not? My Second Uncle has campaigned in the Northwest for many years. If the frontier were truly in trouble, there could not have been no sign of it beforehand." Jiang Yan handed the teacup to Pei Qingyuan, the gaze still as water. Pei Qingyuan gave a faint smile, a deep, meaningful amusement flashing in the eyes. "Tonight, you go sleep in the side hall." The smile on the face of Pei Qingyuan froze at once. The next day, in the name of the senior ministers who upheld the throne, Pei Qingyuan worked together with the Grand Princess to put down the rebellion and steady the court. At the court session, the Grand Princess read out the late emperor's will, passing the throne to the Crown Prince, the late emperor's only son. After seven days of national mourning, the court immediately held the grand enthronement ceremony. And so the little Crown Prince formally became the new Emperor. Jiang Yan also moved to stay temporarily in the Eastern Palace to look after the little Emperor. The following day, while Jiang Yan was busy, the Grand Princess made an unexpected visit, saying a wish to reminisce about old times had brought the visit. Jiang Yan wondered what old times there could be between the two women to reminisce about. "I am leaving the Crown Prince in the care of you and Pei Qingyuan, and my heart is at ease. Once the Crown Prince is on the throne and everything is settled, I will return to my fief. This time I have been away too long, and my family must be worried." The Grand Princess spoke in a light tone, as though having just shed the hard mask worn every day. "But will the young master of the Lu family be worried?" Jiang Yan ventured a bold guess. The Grand Princess looked at Jiang Yan intently and burst into loud laughter: "Did Pei Qingyuan tell you?" Jiang Yan smiled and shook the head: "Your Highness is wearing the finest Song brocade, and the Song brocade of Suzhou Prefecture is the most precious kind. The red jade earrings Your Highness wears were also made by the hand of the most famous artisan in Suzhou. And in Suzhou, only the Lu family possesses a master jade carver like that, and the maternal family of Pei Qingyuan is precisely the Lu family. In the Lu family at present, only the second young master, Lu Jue, is of an age that suits Your Highness." A gleam of admiration lit the eyes of the Grand Princess, who smiled with keen interest: "You are far cleverer than I thought. When did you realize all of this?" "From the moment I wrote to my cousin in Qingzhou." Jiang Yan answered shortly. Ever since the Grand Princess had deliberately sought Jiang Yan out to pick a quarrel, Jiang Yan had been eighty percent certain that this was a play staged for effect. Pei Qingyuan and the Grand Princess had deliberately acted so that everyone would believe the Emperor was gravely ill, while the Grand Princess seized the chance to usurp the throne. Pei Qingyuan joined forces with the court ministers to oppose the Grand Princess. Otherwise, how could the letter Jiang Yan had asked a servant to send to Pei Qingyuan have fallen into the hands of the Grand Princess? This play had been nothing more than a lure to draw out the feudal princes harboring wicked ambitions. Among those princes, the strongest was Prince Duan of the Southwest. "Then are you not afraid that one day he truly fails and the entire board is lost?" The Grand Princess propped up a chin and smiled, probing. "If that day truly comes, I will naturally see to the funeral arrangements for him. I am only afraid that all the civil and military officials together could not match the head full of schemes that Pei Qingyuan has." "Madam Pei is truly deep in her devotion, and I am more grateful than I can say." From the shadows, Pei Qingyuan strolled out unhurriedly, looking at Jiang Yan with eyes brimming with love. Jiang Yan glanced at Pei Qingyuan and did not bother to speak. The Grand Princess laughed softly and, before leaving, tossed out one more line: "I like your temperament very much. If one day you kick Pei Qingyuan out the door, feel free to come to my residence." Pei Qingyuan frowned and immediately waved to see the guest out: "The court is busy. Your Highness had better return quickly." "Are you angry?" Jiang Yan looked coolly at the pretense of obedience from Pei Qingyuan, with not a trace left of the dashing scholar famous throughout the capital. Ten days later, as the court situation gradually settled, Jiang Yan was finally able to leave the palace and return to the Pei Estate. The gates of the Chancellor's Estate now had many more guards posted under strict security. Inside the estate the scenery was peaceful, with only the small figure of the little Emperor sitting on a couch nibbling pastries. It was the first time the little Emperor had left the imperial palace, so everything around seemed wonderfully new. At an age so full of energy, Jiang Yan told Cui Tao to go to the small storeroom and find the dowry chest Jiang Yan had brought: "There are some toys of mine from long ago in there. Bring them out for His Little Highness to try." On hearing this, the little Emperor cheered with delight: "I am going too, I want to see!" Having shed the heavy palace robes, the little Emperor wore clothes made by Jiang Yan's own hands and looked just like a young master of a powerful family, lively yet naturally carrying a noble air. Jiang Yan went on sewing in stillness. Before long the two came back, lugging a small chest between the pair. The little Emperor, hair flushed pink, hugged the chest tight, pulled out a small wooden ball and shook it a few times, and it gave a pleasant "clack clack" sound. Looking up at Jiang Yan, the little Emperor asked eagerly: "Shimu, may I play with this?" Seeing that familiar toy, countless old memories suddenly rose in Jiang Yan's heart. "A-Yan, your body is weak and you cannot train in martial arts. Once I finish learning the red-tasseled spear, I will definitely protect you!" That year, the elder sister had been only ten, dressed in a pale yellow martial robe, hair simply tied high, a bearing so heroic it was like the female knights-errant in the stories Jiang Yan so often heard. The Duke's clan had won its glory through military merit, and its children, male and female, all learned martial arts from childhood. Only Jiang Yan, because of a frail constitution, had to stand and watch brothers and sisters wield spears and cross swords, and could not help feeling wronged inside. Later, Pei Qingyuan came before Jiang Yan holding an exquisite wooden ball. With a teenage boy's pride, the thick brows and bright eyes of Pei Qingyuan shone with a smug smile: "I truly did not expect Third Miss Jiang to have such a gentle, refined little sister. This ball is for you!" Emerging from the flow of memory, Jiang Yan smiled tenderly at the little Emperor: "Go ahead and play." The little Emperor and a close young eunuch promptly dragged each other out to the courtyard to play, clear laughter echoing through the whole place. "Oh, have you finished all of today's lessons?" The deep, warm voice of Pei Qingyuan came from far away. The mouth asked about studies, but the tall figure of Pei Qingyuan had already joined in swiftly, playing with the little Emperor no less eagerly. After a round of merriment, Pei Qingyuan carried the little Emperor, red-faced, back indoors. Never having played so freely before, the little Emperor chattered on, boasting of everything: "Grand Tutor, today Shimu gave me so many new toys!" "Oh, really?" Pei Qingyuan said with a laugh, glancing at Jiang Yan, a meaningful smile tugging up the corner of the mouth. The little Emperor pulled over another chest, nimbly opened the lid, and showed off: "Shimu said these are the things you liked best when you were little! There is a horse pulling a cart, a Lu Ban lock, and even a kite shaped like a rabbit! Grand Tutor, can we go fly kites tomorrow?" Before Jiang Yan could stop the little Emperor from "showing off," Pei Qingyuan was already looking at Jiang Yan with eyes full of amusement. Pei Qingyuan lifted the rabbit kite, examined it for a while, and said: "Since these belong to your Shimu, of course her opinion must be asked." The round eyes of the little Emperor turned at once toward Jiang Yan. Jiang Yan nodded lightly. With permission granted, and afraid the Grand Tutor might think better of it, the little Emperor pulled a book out of the chest: "Grand Tutor, I also found a collection of your writings! Starting tomorrow, I will definitely practice my characters diligently!" Oh? Jiang Yan glanced at Cui Tao. This collection had not been handed over by Pei Qingyuan at all. It was the famous essays Pei Qingyuan had written across the capital over the years, quietly gathered by Jiang Yan and bound into a volume. Cui Tao looked at Jiang Yan with wronged eyes: "Miss, these two chests really look too much alike. His Majesty said these were the Young Master's books and that he wanted to practice his characters, so this servant brought them out without a second thought." Pei Qingyuan set the book down, unhurriedly folded the fan shut, a puzzling smile glittering in the eyes: "So Madam is so fond of these things. In future I will send people to collect more to present to Madam." As for why this collection had been mistaken, it was a long story. Jiang Yan's father, afraid that Jiang Yan and the eldest sister would pick up coarse manners from the elder brothers and later find it hard to marry well, had called on connections to send the two sisters to the Pei Estate for schooling. The Pei family's learning was profound, and Jiang Yan and the sister were the two youngest pupils with much still to learn, so the tutor of the Pei family had Pei Qingyuan teach the two sisters privately. After each day's lesson, the elder sister would dash off at once, preferring to go practice swordplay over sitting to study. But Pei Qingyuan did not mind in the least. Pei Qingyuan always arrived with strange toys in tow, and every stroke of Jiang Yan's characters had been shaped by patient correction. Every toy, every volume of essays: the true feelings Jiang Yan had buried for so many years were now laid bare before Pei Qingyuan, offered up entire with no way to hide. "Shimu, may I?" The little Emperor burrowed into Jiang Yan's arms and asked softly. Jiang Yan rubbed the little Emperor's head and told the young eunuch to take the little Emperor away to rest: "Tomorrow I will play with you, all right?" "Young Master! Urgent news from the Northwest!" With the situation pressing, Qing Zhu hurried in to report, then leaned close and whispered a few words to Pei Qingyuan. Pei Qingyuan nodded: "I understand. You may withdraw." On the way out, Qing Zhu accidentally kicked the chest underfoot. Picking up the wooden ball and looking at it, the boy blurted: "Young Master, is this not the thing you made with your own hands? Why is it here now? Oh, and this kite too. Did you not have to study for half a month with Master Li on Nanbei Street before you could finish it? Back then you said you were making it to give to someone, and it turns out it was for Madam!" Qing Zhu tossed the ball into the chest and, catching the master's gaze darkening, realized too much had slipped out, and turned quickly to Cui Tao: "Cui Tao, it seems Nanny Wang in the kitchen is looking for you!" "Oh, oh, I will go right now!" The two immediately scurried away, leaving Jiang Yan and Pei Qingyuan alone in the room. Jiang Yan walked over, lifted the rabbit kite that had always been the favorite, and said with a smile: "Was it not said that someone would be sent to collect them?" This time Pei Qingyuan was rarely flustered, but Jiang Yan did not need an answer. So all of these things had been made by Pei Qingyuan's own hands. To Jiang Yan, that was more precious than all the countless jade and pearls in the world. The expression on Pei Qingyuan's face changed again and again. In the end, with narrowed eyes, Pei Qingyuan pulled Jiang Yan into a tight embrace and pressed a forceful kiss to the back of Jiang Yan's neck: "Had I known Madam had lost her heart to me so long ago, I would have brought betrothal gifts to your door long since." Jiang Yan lightly pushed against Pei Qingyuan's chest and let out a sigh: "With a tongue this smooth, do not even hope to move from the study back in here." Husband and wife of one heart, and yet Pei Qingyuan had deceived Jiang Yan all this time and meant to brush it off so easily? There was no such good bargain. "If you will not make the first move, then I will! In any case, do not hope to return to Qingzhou, and do not think of that certain cousin anymore!" Pei Qingyuan tightened the arms around Jiang Yan's waist and whispered through gritted teeth beside the ear. Truthfully, Jiang Yan did not want to make the first move. Though Pei Qingyuan had long held a place in the heart, Jiang Yan had no wish to contend for Pei Qingyuan. These true feelings had been buried for years, and had the elder sister not demanded to flee the wedding, Jiang Yan would certainly never have taken this step, and would instead have looked for a chance to leave the capital and keep far from every quarrel. Human beings are greedy without limit. Once this chance had been grasped, Jiang Yan wanted to try once. Having received a small response, Jiang Yan only grew more ambitious and wished for more. "From now on, you are not allowed to take a concubine." "Agreed." Pei Qingyuan had to handle the national mourning and arrange the grand enthronement ceremony, busy for more than a month, and visibly grew thinner. Jiang Yan could not help feeling distressed, personally cooked a few light dishes, and told Cui Tao to carry the dishes to the study for Pei Qingyuan. The study lay on the far side of the compound, where few servants usually attended, since Pei Qingyuan disliked being disturbed. Jiang Yan and Cui Tao entered the courtyard and faintly heard voices inside. Could Pei Qingyuan have a guest? Qing Zhu had not mentioned any visitor beforehand today. Jiang Yan was about to take Cui Tao back when a hearty laugh suddenly rang out. It was the elder sister's voice! Cui Tao started to step forward but was held back by Jiang Yan. The maid looked at Jiang Yan with a face full of grievance: "Miss, let us go back to Qingzhou!" Jiang Yan quietly crept closer to the corner of the neighboring corridor. "Second Young Master Pei, the battle report from the Northwest sent by my third brother has already been delivered by me. You must treat my little sister well. If you dare to wrong her, the whole Jiang family will not let you off!" The elder sister's voice came out clear as day. "Miss Jiang, rest assured. How long do you plan to stay in the capital this time?" Pei Qingyuan replied in a firm voice. "Hmph, how petty! Why not think about who it was that brought about the match between you and my little sister! Give me five hundred taels and I will leave right away!" "If I remember correctly, last time I already gave you a full thousand taels of silver to invite your cooperation, and went to the trouble of sending you out of the capital. How could it be spent so fast?" "Wandering the rivers and lakes is expensive! Three hundred will do!" "Two hundred. Come back once A-Yan and I have a child! That will keep the capital from spreading wild rumors!" "Deal! My fine brother-in-law!" Cui Tao leaned close and asked in a stealthy whisper: "Miss, are we still going back to Qingzhou?" Jiang Yan only smiled and lightly shook the head. There was no such place as Qingzhou to go to. Contact with the cousin in Qingzhou had ceased long ago. The share of the dowry in Qingzhou had also been exchanged for silver taels long since and carried close at hand. Cui Tao truly was a silly girl. While held in the palace, any letters sent out would surely have been inspected strictly, and Jiang Yan had never intended to send a letter at all. The letter, the chest of keepsakes, all of it had been deliberately placed by Jiang Yan to appear at just the right place and just the right time. There was no such thing as a match fated by heaven. All of it came from calculation, step by step, and scheming in silence.
