At the ferry landing where the two parted, Ji Shuyan pulled Bai Nianqiu close and whispered a promise: "When the willows shade the lake next spring, I will surely ride home on a tall horse to fetch you." People whispered that Ji Shuyan was wanton by nature, keeping courtesans of both sexes in the rear courtyard. Hearing this, Bai Nianqiu only smiled faintly. Ji Shuyan had gone to no small trouble to escape the pestering of powerful families eager to marry off their daughters, not hesitating even to wreck a good name.
