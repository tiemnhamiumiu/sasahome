Later Princess Xian explained to Yanru that the singing had been practice, and that it happened to be the stage of “stretching the voice.” That same night, Princess Xian had been unable to hold back before Xiao Kaiguang’s good looks, and had let the plan slip to Xiao Kaiguang by accident. Xiao Kaiguang, in a towering rage, ran to Kunning Palace and threatened to depose Yanru as Empress. Xiao Kaiguang had been sulking at the time, and Yanru took even the words blurted out in that sulk as material for the plan. Yanru truly was a calamity-bringing beauty of exceptionally high intelligence. On that day of standing in the white snow, Yanru knew perfectly well that Xiao Kaiguang missed Yanru very much, and Yanru missed Xiao Kaiguang just as much. Xiao Kaiguang had told Yanru not to reach too deep into this matter, and Yanru had deliberately bitten down hard on Xiao Kaiguang’s shoulder to deliver the poison, in order to control Xiao Kaiguang, to make Xiao Kaiguang lie obediently unconscious and not obstruct Yanru’s work. Now Yanru could say with confidence that Yanru had never once regretted marrying Xiao Kaiguang. Back then, it was Yanru who had personally asked Xiao Ruoyu to bring Xiao Kaiguang along. From beginning to end, the only one Yanru had ever secretly yearned for was Xiao Kaiguang, yet Xiao Kaiguang had stubbornly insisted on believing that Yanru was always pining for Xiao Ruoyu. The storm had now fully subsided, and the scene was filled with the beautiful colors of spring. The Crown Prince returned smoothly to the palace. The Crown Prince entered Zhengyang Hall to pay respects to the Emperor, and instead came upon Yanru, the Crown Prince’s own mother, standing obediently with the face to the wall just outside the hall door. Worried, the Crown Prince came close, taking it for another quarrel between the parents. Yanru said gently: “Do not be afraid, it is a small matter. Your father and I are playing the game of chasing the husband to the crematorium.” Yanru went on: “Right now your father is blaming me for poisoning him, and says he regrets marrying me.” Xiao Kaiguang’s voice came at once from inside the hall: “Did I ever say that?” Listen to that. “Did I ever say that?” What a magnificent line. It had been over a month since Xiao Kaiguang had been willing to speak a word to Yanru. Yanru was so moved as to nearly weep. Pushing the Crown Prince out the door, Yanru instructed: “My good son, your parents have conquered this realm for you. Consolidating and maintaining the country is for you to rely on from now on. Go, go, work hard. An inch of time is an inch of gold, and a ruler’s son must learn early to manage the affairs of the state.” The Crown Prince nodded obediently and sprinted off until vanished from sight. Yanru hurried into the hall and shut the door tightly behind. Xiao Kaiguang was reading memorials and said unhurriedly: “Why do you keep deceiving him all day?” “Children are easily tricked when young, so that as grown they are not easily tricked by others.” Yanru went on: “Otherwise, once some pretty girl came to lure him, off he would go at once, and how could he ever shoulder the great enterprise?” Xiao Kaiguang must have been reminded of having willingly followed a beautiful girl once, and bowed the head with a laugh: “All sophistry.” Pressing the advantage, Yanru hurried close and asked in a low voice: “You are not angry with me anymore, are you?” Yanru explained: “That medicine actually has little toxicity. It was sold by my dance teacher. Her super-potent aphrodisiac did not sell, so afterward she switched to selling tonic pills along with fake poisons that will not kill anyone.” Xiao Kaiguang’s hand stopped on the brush: “What super-potent aphrodisiac?” Oh no. A slip of the tongue again. “Chu Yanru!” Xiao Kaiguang glared at Yanru. “You once told me your teacher was a lady from a good family fallen on hard times, forced to sell her art but not her body, and then she used true talent to pass the examination into the Bureau of Rites and Music, did you not? A perfectly respectable person, you said!” Yanru raised the head and stared up at the rafters, pretending not to hear. Xiao Kaiguang went on: “And another thing, am I angry at you over the poisoning?” Yanru was very sensible about it and spoke up at once: “How could that be? You are angry that I walked into danger on my own without telling you beforehand, is that it?” Xiao Kaiguang gave a soft hum in the throat. Seeing that the situation seemed to be developing very favorably, Yanru drew close with great attentiveness and whispered: “Tonight may I come to your bedchamber and spread a mat to sleep?” Xiao Kaiguang answered at once, without needing any time to think: “No.” Now that the harem had officially been dissolved and the sisters-in-law had settled down to pursue private dreams, Xiao Kaiguang had returned to being a serious and proper man. Thinking of the snowfall of that year, and then of the sisters-in-law, Yanru realized that now there were not even enough people left to gather for mahjong. Yanru played the final trump card: “We can begin thinking about having a daughter now.” But Xiao Kaiguang still flatly refused to fall for it. Men truly were creatures very hard to coax. Yanru rolled the eyes in discouragement and reached out to grind ink for Xiao Kaiguang. When all the work had been dealt with, Xiao Kaiguang looked up at Yanru and said unhurriedly: “Can you not simply lie down properly on the bed to sleep?” Oh? There was hope! Yanru immediately answered with a bright smile: “Of course I can!”
