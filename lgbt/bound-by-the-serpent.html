Thinking of this, Ethan asked cautiously: "You don't blame me for pushing your cultivation off course, do you?" Elias smiled: "At first, of course I was a little displeased." Elias took Ethan's hand and pressed it lightly to Elias's chest. "But being human has its own charm. Otherwise, how could I have met you, how could I have become your boyfriend?" Elias told Ethan that on Ethan's first day of enrollment, the day Ethan had been convinced was their first meeting, Elias had instantly recognized Ethan as the child from long ago. At first Elias had meant only to pay Ethan a little extra attention, but who could have guessed that a beauty-loving person like Ethan would fall for Elias at first sight and chase without letting go. Truly an arrangement of fate. Hearing this, Ethan cut in: "Are you saying that means you love me? That's too easy, too shallow!" Destiny, fate, what nonsense, Ethan didn't believe in any of that! Ethan fretted aloud: "Could it be that you don't love me for being handsome, lively, and gifted in both looks and talent?" Elias, rarely, burst into loud laughter. Ethan pinched Elias hard in a huff: "No laughing! I'm not convinced! I'm angry!" Elias looked at Ethan tenderly: "Then what must I do before you stop being angry?" Ethan thought for a while and decided to use the chance to claim a little fairness. "I chased you for three months straight, so starting today, you have to be the one chasing me!" "And while you're at it, think carefully about what 'love' actually is. Once your answer satisfies me, only then are we done!" "If you can't find an answer, it means you never truly loved me!" Elias didn't mind Ethan's spoiled, fussy manner in the least. Instead, Elias took Ethan's hand, lifted it to the lips, and pressed a gentle kiss on the back of it. "As you command." In a mere forty-eight hours, Ethan's life had been violently turned upside down. The celibate boyfriend had suddenly turned into a snake demon! And not just any snake demon, but one bound to Ethan by destiny since childhood. But however dramatic and absurd the situation, Ethan still had to drag free of Elias's arms and heave a backpack up onto the shoulders to go to class. Who said Ethan wasn't still an overworked college student! Before leaving Elias's dorm room, Ethan suddenly remembered something important. "You said that in just a few months your cultivation would break through, right?" Elias nodded slightly. Ethan hesitated, as if finding it hard to say: "You also said that by then the two of us would… become one?" Elias instantly caught the implication and curved the lips in a smirk: "That's right. What, are you disappointed?" Ethan glared in warning. "Oh." Elias stroked the chin, pretending to ponder. "You're making me chase you, so before I succeed, intimate activities shouldn't take place, right?" Elias sighed as if facing a great difficulty: "I wonder whether I'll manage to catch you before my cultivation is complete." Ethan hurriedly protested: "Those two things are completely different!" Elias looked at Ethan with great interest. Ethan realized another trap had been set, but still insisted stubbornly: "Chasing me is a mandatory obligation! But satisfying my needs is also a responsibility! It counts as part of the courting process too!" Elias gently pinched Ethan's cheek and said meaningfully: "Do you prefer the snake form?" Ethan's face went hot, and Ethan mumbled: "It's so-so. Quantity doesn't matter. Technique is what counts." Elias tilted Ethan's chin up, leaned close, and whispered: "To improve technique, you need real combat practice. Want to practice a few more rounds?" "No way!" Ethan instantly leapt back two steps, arms crossed tightly over the chest. "I already skipped class this morning! I absolutely can't skip this afternoon too!" With that, Ethan fled like a gust of wind. Elias's delighted laughter echoed behind. Infuriating! A person who suppresses desire too much is no good, but a person with too much desire is no good either! For the next three months, Elias followed Ethan's demand and began the formal pursuit. Eating together, going to class together, joining activities together, the very model of a modern boyfriend, appearing at once whenever Ethan needed. All of it left Ethan's three roommates gaping in astonishment. "Ethan Hale, where did you learn such supreme boyfriend-managing skills?!" Ethan was thrilled inside but kept up a modest front: "It's nothing special, really." But each time Ethan asked, "So have you figured out what 'love' is yet?", the answer still failed to fully satisfy. For that reason, in name Elias remained only Ethan's "probationary boyfriend." Naturally, this probation period had no effect whatsoever on the intimate activities between Ethan and Elias. That counted as an important part of the trial too! Then came Ethan's birthday, when Elias said Elias wanted to take Ethan to a special place. Ethan happily agreed. Elias gently covered Ethan's eyes, and Ethan only felt a light swirl of air around the body. When the hand lifted away, the two of them stood atop a towering mountain peak. Ethan looked around, bewildered: "Where is this? Your hometown?" Elias didn't answer, only pulled Ethan down onto a large boulder. Ethan understood nothing: "What are we doing?" "Waiting." "For what?" "The sunset here is beautiful." Elias tilted the head and smiled at Ethan. "I cultivated here for many years and have watched the sunset here ten thousand times. "Until a few days ago, when I came back to retrieve a cultivation tool from those years. "By chance I saw that sunset again, and suddenly I wanted you beside me, with an aching longing." Aha. Ethan broke into a radiant smile too. Alright then. That cold-blooded creature, that snake-demon senior, that probationary boyfriend of Ethan's. Elias had at last come to understand what "love" truly meant. Someday, a person will meet someone by chance. That someone's arrival will awaken every sense within a person. As if a person were being given a pair of entirely new eyes with which to look at this world. Mountains rolling on and on, cool flowing streams, a passing breeze, clouds drifting silently, or the glow of dusk settling down. Everything in the world will carry brand-new meaning. A person will always want to share each of those beautiful moments with that someone. Wherever the heart turns, that place is where love overflows.
